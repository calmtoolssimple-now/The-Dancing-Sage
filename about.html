About The Dancing Sage

A gentle place for women who have spent years caring, working, carrying responsibilities, and sometimes losing sight of themselves along the way.

A note from Collette

I've been there, and I want to help

Hi, I'm Collette, and I'd like to take a moment to introduce myself.

For more than 25 years I worked in technology. Somewhere between the corporate climb and the everyday, I got lost. There were difficulties with family. After 45, my body stopped working the way it always had. Food started to feel like the enemy, and I finally had to give up my glass of wine.

Maybe some of that sounds familiar. From 40 onward, so many of us go through a stretch of crazy: the mood swings, the restlessness, the feeling that something is missing, and a quiet despair we don't always talk about.

I've been through it, and I'm still here. That's why I created The Dancing Sage: to do my very best to help women living through the same season, with honest encouragement, a little humour and small, doable ways to feel like yourself again.

I'm here, I've been there, and I want to help.

Lots of warm thoughts,
Collette

Why She Was Created

The Dancing Sage began with a simple belief: women do not become less valuable as they grow older. They carry experience, humour, tenderness, strength, and wisdom that deserve to be seen and celebrated.

She was created by Collette, a Canadian woman who spent many years building a career, caring for others, and learning firsthand how easily stress can crowd out the quieter parts of life. The Dancing Sage grew from a desire to help other women pause, breathe, and remember that they still matter too.

What You Will Find Here

This website offers a free calm-reset tool, original reflections, gentle practices, books, journals, and creative resources. Everything is intended to make self-care feel approachable—not like another demanding task.

The person and the character

Collette is the creator behind The Dancing Sage. The silver-haired Sage is an illustrated character and storytelling voice, created to offer a welcoming place for reflection and creativity.

How to use this website

Begin with a reflection in the Wisdom Library, try the everyday activity beneath it, or follow one of the colouring guides. These resources are available without a purchase or email address. The calm and joy tools select from prepared suggestions based on your choices; they are not a live conversation or a professional assessment.

Books and optional email gifts are clearly labelled separately. Questions or corrections are welcome through the Contact page.

Our Guiding Values

  • Kindness toward ourselves and one another
  • Calm that can fit into ordinary life
  • Respect for women in every season of life
  • Simple joy, creativity, nature, and gentle movement
  • Honest encouragement without promises of perfection

A gentle note: The Dancing Sage provides general wellness and self-care information. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, therapy, or a substitute for professional care.
