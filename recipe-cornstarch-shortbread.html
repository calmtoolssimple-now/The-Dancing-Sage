Cornstarch Shortbread

Shortbread cookies, not hockey pucks! We're baking for Christmas, not the NHL. This is my family's melt-in-your-mouth cornstarch shortbread, the recipe I make every year, mixed by hand with those familiar little fork marks on top.

  • 300°F (150°C)
  • About 20 min bake
  • Makes about 4 dozen

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups Becel Vegan margarine sticks
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup icing sugar
  • 1 cup cornstarch
  • ¼ teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 300°F (150°C).
  2. Combine all the ingredients in a large bowl. Mix by hand until they come together into a soft dough.
  3. Roll the dough into 1-inch balls and place them on a baking sheet.
  4. Gently flatten each ball with a fork.
  5. Bake for about 20 minutes. Keep them pale rather than waiting for a golden-brown top.
  6. Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet before moving them. They are delicate while warm!
🌿 Sage's Tip

Pale is perfect. The moment shortbread turns golden it starts heading toward hockey-puck territory. And if yours turn out crumbly, here's how to fix it.

Good to know

Why cornstarch?

Cornstarch replaces some of the flour and makes the dough more tender, so the cookies melt in your mouth instead of being crisp or crunchy. It's the secret behind that soft, delicate texture people remember.

Butter or margarine?

This version uses Becel Vegan margarine sticks, which makes it dairy-free. You can use the same amount of salted or unsalted butter for a classic butter shortbread. If you use unsalted butter, keep the salt in the recipe.

Storing

Keep cooled shortbread in an airtight tin at room temperature for up to 2 weeks. Separate the layers with parchment or wax paper so they don't stick or break.

Freezing

Baked shortbread freezes very well for up to 3 months. You can also freeze the rolled dough balls on a tray, then pack them in a freezer bag and bake straight from frozen, adding a few extra minutes.

Questions people ask

Why is my shortbread crumbly?

Usually there's a little too much flour or cornstarch, or the dough wasn't mixed long enough to come together. Measure by spooning the flour into the cup and levelling it off rather than scooping. The Sage has a whole letter on fixing crumbly shortbread.

Why did my shortbread spread or go flat?

The dough was too warm. If your kitchen is warm, chill the dough balls for 15 minutes before baking. More help is in why cookies spread flat.

How do I know when shortbread is done?

The tops stay pale and the bottoms are just barely golden. Shortbread firms up as it cools, so it will still seem soft when you take it out of the oven.
