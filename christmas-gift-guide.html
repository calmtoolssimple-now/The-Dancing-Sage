A note from the Sage

The best Christmas gifts aren't the most expensive ones. They're the ones that get used every year: the teapot that comes out every December, the plate that holds the shortbread, the blanket that ends up on the same chair every evening. Choose one thing that will be loved for a long time rather than five things that will be forgotten by February.

Not sure what to get your mother? Look at what's already on her table. A matching piece for a pattern she loves, or the teapot she's always admired, means far more than something new and shiny.
