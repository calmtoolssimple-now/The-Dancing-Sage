Contact The Dancing Sage

Questions, thoughtful notes, and kind conversation are always welcome.

Email

The easiest way to reach The Dancing Sage is by email: hello@askthedancingsage.com. I read every message and reply as soon as I can, usually within a few days.

Have a question for the advice column? Send it to Ask the Sage instead, so it can be answered on the website.

What to write to the Sage about

The advice column welcomes everyday questions from readers of every age: a tricky family moment, a friendship that has gone quiet, a manners dilemma, a recipe that keeps letting you down, or a big "what now?" after the children leave home or work winds down.

When you send a question for the column, please leave out real names and anything that could identify you or anyone else. If your question is chosen, it may be shortened or lightly edited, and it will be published without your name. The Sage reads every question but can't reply to each one personally.

For group leaders and libraries

If you run activities for a seniors' residence, a 50+ club or a library, you are welcome to print the Sage's free colouring pages for your group. The Colouring & Tea group activity has a ready-to-run one-hour plan, and you can email with any questions about using it.
