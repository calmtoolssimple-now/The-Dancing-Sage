A Letter to the Tired Woman

A soft reminder for the woman who has been holding too much for too long.

← Back to the Wisdom Library

Dear tired woman,

I see you, even in the moments you think no one is looking. The ones where you finally sit down and your shoulders drop half an inch, just before you remember the next thing waiting for you.

You have been carrying so much for so long that carrying has started to feel like your permanent shape. You forget, some days, that you are allowed to set something down.

You are not tired because you are weak. You are tired because you have been strong for longer than anyone should have to be, in more places than anyone noticed, for more people than ever thought to ask if you were okay.

You do not need to earn your rest by first collapsing. You do not need permission from anyone else to stop, even for a moment, even before everything is finished.

It will not all get done today. It didn't all get done yesterday either, and somehow you are still here, still standing, still showing up in the ways that matter most.

That is not failure. That is simply what it looks like to be one woman carrying a life that was never meant to be carried entirely alone.

So today, tired woman, let this be your permission. Put one thing down. Just one. Let it wait. Let someone else worry about it, or let it simply be unfinished for a while.

You are allowed to rest before you are completely empty. You are allowed to be tired without also being endlessly capable at the exact same moment.

Rest now, if only for a little while. The rest of it will still be there. And so, gently, will you.

With tenderness,

— The Dancing Sage
