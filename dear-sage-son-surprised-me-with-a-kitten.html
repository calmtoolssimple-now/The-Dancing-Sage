Dear Sage,

My adult son surprised me with a kitten. I don't want a cat! I want to go out when I please. How do I tell him?

— Hoping for Chocolates

In short: Thank him for the thought, then tell him kindly and clearly that you won't be keeping a pet. Ask him to help find the kitten a good home through whoever it came from or a reputable rescue, and keep it safe and cared for until then.

The Sage replies…

Darling, a kitten is a commitment with whiskers. That requires a conversation—not a surprise delivery.

Tell him kindly: "I know you meant well, but I'm not willing to take on a pet. We need to arrange a suitable home for this little one."

Ask him to help contact whoever the kitten came from, or a reputable rescue. Until then, make sure it's safe and cared for.

You can appreciate the thought and still say no.

And tell him next year, something that doesn't need a litter tray.

With a wink and a twirl,
— The Dancing Sage 🌿
