About Ask The Dancing Sage

The Dancing Sage is a warm, silver-haired guide with a fondness for tea, a soft spot for anyone who has spent years looking after everyone else, and a habit of twirling in the kitchen. Her answers cover:

  • Family: Grown children, in-laws, holidays and keeping the peace.
  • Friendship & Love: Partners, friends and finding your people at any age.
  • Etiquette & Manners: The gracious thing to do, without the stuffiness.
  • Baking & the Kitchen: Flat cookies, festive baking and kitchen comfort.
  • Life After 45: Who am I now? Starting again, and enjoying it.
  • Life's Little Puzzles: Mystery keys, odd family traditions and everyday head-scratchers.

The first answers here respond to questions women ask the Sage again and again. Questions sent in by readers are marked "A reader asks."

The Dancing Sage offers friendly, everyday advice, not professional counselling, medical, legal or financial advice. For anything serious, ongoing or urgent, please reach out to a doctor, counsellor or someone you trust. If you or someone else may be in danger, contact local emergency services.
