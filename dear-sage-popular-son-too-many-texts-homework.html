Dear Dancing Sage,

My son has several girls calling and texting him constantly! I know he's popular, but all this attention is distracting him from school. Popular or not, he has to study! What should I do?

— Mum of Mr. Popular

In short: He doesn't need to be less popular, just less available during homework. Homework first, chatting afterward, and the phone parks in another room during study time.

The Sage replies…

Dear Mum,

Ah, young love. Apparently it now comes with unlimited notifications.

Less available, not less popular

Your son doesn't need to become less popular. He needs to become less available during homework.

Homework first, chatting afterward

Give him a timeline: homework first, chatting afterward. During study time, park the phone in another room. It's much harder to concentrate when the table keeps buzzing.

He's in charge of his homework

Keep the conversation about his responsibilities. The girls aren't in charge of his homework. He is.

And remind him: "Anyone worth your time can wait while you finish the math homework."

Warmly,
— The Dancing Sage
