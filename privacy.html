Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information The Dancing Sage may collect, why it is collected, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Information You Choose to Provide

If you subscribe to receive a journal, reset, newsletter, or other email, you may provide your email address and related subscription preferences. Email subscriptions are managed through MailerLite and are used only for the purpose described when you sign up. You may unsubscribe through the link included in any email.

Website Usage Information

This website uses Google Analytics to understand general website activity, such as which pages are visited, approximate location, device or browser type, and how visitors arrive at the site. This information helps improve the website and is not used by The Dancing Sage to identify you personally.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Google Analytics, MailerLite, GitHub Pages, and linked third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies for functionality, security, measurement, and preferences. Where required, visitors will be given choices about non-essential cookies.

Advertising

This website may display advertising in the future through Google AdSense. If advertising is activated, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show, measure, and personalize ads, subject to your consent choices and applicable law. This policy and the website's consent controls will be updated as needed when advertising is enabled.

Affiliate Links and Purchases

Some links are affiliate links, including links to Amazon. If you make a qualifying purchase, Calm & Simple may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you. Purchases made through Amazon, Gumroad, or another external store are governed by that provider's own privacy and payment policies. This website does not directly receive or store your payment-card information.

External Links

The website links to services such as Amazon, Gumroad, Pinterest, Facebook, Instagram, Medium, and MailerLite. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and The Dancing Sage is not responsible for the content or privacy practices of external websites.

Data Retention and Your Choices

Subscription information is retained while you remain subscribed or as needed to meet legal and operational requirements. You may unsubscribe at any time. You may also use your browser settings to delete or block cookies, although some website features may then work differently.

Children's Privacy

The Dancing Sage website is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised when website features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page will show when the policy was most recently revised.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use one of the official contact methods listed on the Contact page.
